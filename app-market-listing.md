## Features

- Add responsive comments and review widgets to your site.
- Manage replies, reactions, ratings, and testimonials in one place.
- Moderate submissions with approve, reject, pin, delete, and owner-reply actions.
- Customize widget content, layout, colors, typography, and display settings.
- Support guest and member posting with configurable moderation controls.

## Full description

Engage Comments gives site owners a flexible way to collect and display comments, reviews, ratings, and testimonials. Add a responsive widget to your site, customize its appearance to match your brand, and give visitors a clear way to share feedback.

Manage conversations from a single dashboard with threaded replies, reactions, sorting, pagination, moderation controls, and owner responses. Enable star ratings for review-focused widgets, feature selected testimonials, and configure the experience for guests or signed-in members.

Built with accessibility, privacy, and dependable moderation in mind, Engage Comments helps you keep visitor feedback organized while creating more opportunities for meaningful conversations on your site.
